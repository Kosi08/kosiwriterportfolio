01 SHORT-FORM VIDEO SCRIPT

THE MOST BIZZARE WAYS PEOPLE HAVE DIED

A short-form storytelling script designed to capture attention immediately and maintain curiosity throughout the story.

SCRIPT

In 1184, some of the most powerful men in medieval Germany gathered in Erfurt for a royal meeting

Nobles, bishops, and other important men were packed onto an upper floor.

And then...

The floor collapsed.
But it didn't stop there.
The floor below collapsed too.

And dozens of men fell straight into a massive cesspit filled with human waste.
Some were crushed by the falling debris.
Others drowned in the filth.
Around 60 men died.

And somehow, King Henry VI survived.
Because he was sitting in a stone window alcove.

Apparently, even in medieval Germany...
being the king came with some very good seating.
The survivors were eventually pulled from the wreckage.

But imagine gathering the most powerful men in the kingdom for an important political meeting...
Only for the entire building to decide it was time to...
flush.

And that's how one of the strangest disasters in medieval history became known as...

The Erfurt Latrine Disaster.

02 SHORT-FORM VIDEO SCRIPT

I ATE MY FRIEND WHO WAS REPORTED MISSING

A fictional horror story script written for a TikTok horror content creator.

SCRIPT

I was fifteen when I found out my mom was mentally ill.
I only found out because she was arrested for kidnapping and murdering children in our neighbourhood.
One of those children was my best friend, Rick.

Rick and I were supposed to meet at the town park one day when we were twelve, but he never showed up.
A search party was organized, and they searched for a month, but nothing was ever found.
I cried almost every night for a very long time, but my mom just said everything would be okay, that Rick would "return to us."

We were poor, and my mom only worked shifts, but there was always so much meat in our meals.
I attended the town's free community school, and I'd always come home to food that was mostly meat.

I didn't think much of it at the time.
Not until I found the friendship bracelet I'd forced Rick to wear in my mom's drawer.
I remember staring at it for a long time, but I still didn't think she'd hurt him.
My mom had always been a little creepy, but I never thought she was capable of doing anything like that.

One day, two years after Rick's disappearance, I got intense menstrual cramps at school and had to go home early.
From a distance, I saw my mom getting into her car.
She was wearing an old tank top and shorts, and in the back seat was the neighbour's three-year-old daughter.

She didn't see me.

I didn't understand what was happening.
I just knew something was wrong.

My mom came home late that night carrying three plastic bags containing meat.
She told me she'd gotten it on a flash sale.

The next morning, the neighbour's daughter was reported missing.
I didn't know what to do.
I was terrified of what my mom might do if she realized I knew.
So I told her I was becoming vegetarian.
I stopped eating anything she cooked.
She didn't seem to care.
She just kept bringing home meat.

Then another child went missing.
And another.
And another.
One day, I came home to the police in my house.
They had found evidence linking my mom to the missing children.

Lots of it.

My mom insisted she thought she was hunting animals.
She genuinely seemed to believe it.
She pleaded insanity and was locked away in a maximum-security psychiatric unit.

I thought it was over.

Until two weeks ago.
My mom escaped.

She's still out there somewhere.

And this time, I know exactly what she's hunting for.
